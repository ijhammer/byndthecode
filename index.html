On Beyond the Code, Yitzy Hammer goes backstage with the founders, lawmakers, regulators, and legal minds shaping the frontier of AI, crypto, and blockchain — and the laws racing to catch up.

Each conversation digs into two things: the origin story that got them here, and the hard questions no one has clean answers to yet. Where is the technology actually heading? Who's writing the rules, and what happens when the rules don't exist yet? What does it take to build responsibly when the ground keeps shifting under everyone's feet?

The goal is to surface the untold decisions, the lessons learned the hard way, and the insights that make the work sharper — for Yitzy, and for everyone listening.

Yitzy Hammer is a crypto and blockchain lawyer at DLT LAW, one of Israel's leading digital assets practices. For over seven years he has been advising exchanges, protocols, funds, and founders navigating the regulatory frontier across emerging tech sectors.

Over 100 episodes in, Beyond the Code has become a home for the people actually building, regulating, and enforcing the future of digital assets — founders, compliance officers, law enforcement, policymakers, and the lawyers who keep it all from falling apart.
